How to lay out the sheet

Put these column names in the first row. Only first_name and visit_date are required.

first_name other_names phone residence visit_date service born_again prayer_points prayer_categories sms_consent notes

Dates can be 2026-10-04 or 04/10/2026. Born again can be yes, no or not sure. Visits older than {{ settings.followup_weeks|int + 2 }} weeks are saved without sending reminders.
